# ADR-0006: Readiness Is Signal-Based (Five Markers), Not Turn-Counter

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-10
- **Authority:** Founders
- **Supersedes:** Earlier turn-counter approach (not individually documented; this ADR
  is the canonical record of the replacement)

## Context

An earlier version of THE ELDER's diagnostic architecture used a turn-counter to
determine when a Reading was ready to be offered: after N exchanges, the session
proceeded to generation. This approach was mechanical, ignored the actual quality of
evidence present in the session, and produced Readings for sessions where the seeker
had provided little meaningful signal — an instrument that spoke before it had listened.

The replacement approach treats readiness as an evidence question: the session emits
the ⟡⟡READY⟡⟡ signal only when sufficient markers have fired, regardless of how many
turns have elapsed.

## Decision

Readiness in THE ELDER is determined exclusively by the signal-based diagnostic:
five evidentiary markers — **Wound, Figure, Threshold, Exile, Pattern** — tracked
across the session. The ⟡⟡READY⟡⟡ signal fires when the evidence warrants a Reading;
it does not fire on a schedule. Turn count is never a readiness criterion.

The markers are defined as:
- **Wound** — a named hurt, loss, rupture, or unresolved grief present in the seeker's material
- **Figure** — a person, archetype, or relational force that carries charge in the telling
- **Threshold** — a decision, crossing, or liminal moment explicitly or implicitly present
- **Exile** — something cast out, suppressed, unacknowledged, or not yet integrated
- **Pattern** — a recurring dynamic, compulsion, or mythic structure the seeker demonstrates

## Consequences

- No prompt, contract revision, or UI change may introduce turn-count gating without
  superseding this ADR.
- The marker definitions above are the canonical rubric for inter-rater reliability
  work (strategy v2/v3 Phase 2 research design).
- A session may be long and still not reach ⟡⟡READY⟡⟡ if evidence is thin; the voice
  continues listening. This is by design and should be communicated to users as a
  feature of the instrument's honesty, not a bug.
- The five markers are also the foundation of the altar record schema's `markers_fired`
  JSONB field (schema.sql) and the pre/post measurement instrument for Phase 2.

## Provenance

The shift from turn-counter to signal-based was a major architecture decision made
during Council of Voices build-out. The five marker names have been stable since that
session and are referenced throughout strategy v1–v3.
