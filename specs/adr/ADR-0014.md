# ADR-0014: PROPOSED — Focus The Elder on the Maya diaspora

- **Status:** proposed — OPEN, pending lineage review and sign-off
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Authority:** Founders (proposal). Lineage direction attributed to Vincent J. Stanzione as relayed by the project owner; **no written approval from him is on record for this ADR** (see Provenance).
- **Supersedes:** — (does not change ADR-0007 or ADR-0003; see Consequences)

## Context

THE ELDER currently presents as a multi-tradition Council (K'iche' Ojer Tzij, Norse, Greek, Vedic, Sufi, Mekubal and others), each voice bound by ADR-0007 to its own field. The K'iche' Maya voice is one among many.

The project owner has stated the purpose that motivates the work: to build a living link with the Mesoamerican diaspora, specifically the Maya people who left Guatemala from 1954 onward because of the internal conflict, and who now live in the United States. The view relayed is that The Elder has lost power by being spread across many traditions, and that focusing it would make it much more powerful.

This is a product-purpose question and a lineage question. It is not an engineering ticket, and it should not be decided by a silent change to prompts, defaults or copy.

## Decision (proposed)

**The Elder's primary purpose is to serve the K'iche' Maya diaspora and its descendants, with the K'iche' (Ojer Tzij) voice as the center of the instrument.** Other Council voices remain governed by ADR-0007 and are not removed by this ADR; their prominence, defaults and framing are the subject of the open questions below.

## Open questions (for the owner, then lineage review)

1. **Who is the seeker?** Maya-descended people in the US only, or anyone, with the diaspora as the center of gravity? This decides defaults, copy and onboarding.
2. **What happens to the other voices?** Options: (a) keep all, K'iche' leads by default; (b) keep as secondary "other roads"; (c) retire from the public surface. Retiring a voice supersedes nothing in ADR-0007 but needs its own ADR.
3. **Language.** Spanish and K'iche' alongside English for diaspora seekers. The app has a `LanguageToggle`; which languages are in scope, and who reviews K'iche' text?
4. **Which community?** The 1954 displacement and later waves (1960-1996) involved K'iche', Kaqchikel, Mam, Q'eqchi', Ixil and others. ADR-0003/0004/0008 and the corpus are K'iche'-specific. Does "Maya diaspora" mean K'iche' only, or does it need voices from other Maya communities with their own accountable reviewers?
5. **Welfare and trauma.** Displacement and violence are part of this community's history. The crisis and distress directives (`check:crisis-directive`, age-register spec) need review against this audience before any launch.
6. **Who speaks for the community?** Beyond one translator, which Ajq'ij or community bodies in the diaspora review the voice and the copy?

## Consequences (if accepted)

- Copy (`README.md`, `app/about/page.tsx`, landing) would state the diaspora purpose. New seeker-facing text needs the owner's approval of wording.
- `LineageSelector` default and ordering would favor Ojer Tzij.
- Corpus work would prioritize K'iche' sources and, if scope widens, other Maya communities with named reviewers.
- Any prompt change to the Ojer Tzij voice goes through the existing sign-off process in `governance/signoffs/`.
- Nothing in this ADR changes behavior until it is accepted. No code is touched by this proposal.
