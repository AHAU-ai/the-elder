# ADR-0014: PROPOSED — The Maya diaspora purpose belongs to La Baluka, not The Elder

- **Status:** proposed — OPEN, pending owner confirmation and lineage sign-off
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Authority:** Founders (proposal). Direction attributed to Vincent J. Stanzione as relayed by the project owner; **no written approval from him is on record for this ADR** (see Provenance).
- **Supersedes:** — (replaces this ADR's own earlier draft, which scoped the diaspora purpose to The Elder; that draft was never accepted)

## Context

The project owner stated a purpose: a living link with the Mesoamerican diaspora, specifically the Maya who left Guatemala from 1954 onward because of the internal conflict and settled in the United States. The view relayed, as Vincent Stanzione's, is that a focused instrument would be much more powerful, and that the spread of the current one costs it power.

The owner clarified that what Vinny is describing is **La Baluka**. In this repo La Baluka is a separate product from The Elder (`governance/checklist.yaml`, tier groups LB TIER 1 and 2):

- **K'iche'-primary.** The K'iche' text is the product, not a translation (LB-004).
- **Natal core.** Cruz Maya natal rendering and Venus-phase dimension (LB-002, LB-003).
- **Frozen by policy** until The Elder's container seal; unfreeze criteria are LB-001.
- It reuses The Elder's gates: welfare and crisis hard-block (LB-005), the single-door publication gate (ADR-0013).

The Elder is, by ADR-0007, a multi-tradition Council. A single-community diaspora focus is a different product, not a narrowing of this one.

## Decision (proposed)

**The Maya-diaspora purpose is La Baluka's. The Elder is not refocused.** The Elder remains the multi-tradition Council under ADR-0007 and is the foundation (container, gates, corpus pipeline) that La Baluka builds on.

## Consequences

- No Elder code, copy, prompt or default changes. Nothing in the `/the-elder` product narrows to one community.
- The diaspora purpose should be written into La Baluka's own scope document and checklist rows, not into Elder copy.
- Sequencing is unchanged: La Baluka stays frozen until LB-001's seal criteria are met; the Elder work that matters most to the diaspora is finishing that seal (ELD-066, GK-007 and drift contexts required on main, welfare gate ELD-073).
- Questions for the La Baluka scope (not decided here): who the seeker is (US diaspora only, or wider); other Maya communities displaced in the same period (Kaqchikel, Mam, Q'eqchi', Ixil) and who reviews for them; Spanish and K'iche' alongside English; welfare handling for a community with a history of violence and displacement; which Ajq'ij or diaspora bodies review the voice and copy.
