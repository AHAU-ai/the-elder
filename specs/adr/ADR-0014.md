# ADR-0014: Return-Visit Reflection (R1) — the seeker's own two lines

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Authority:** Founders (Appendix B dependency OPEN: Shalom Ormsby to confirm)
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

Returning seekers already have structural recurrence (ADR on trajectory, migrations 009/020), but
it speaks only through the Elder's voice and only as static stage clauses. The founders want a
returning seeker to be able to see their own life from a slightly different angle without the
instrument telling them what they are. The least new machinery that does this: show the seeker,
verbatim and dated, the first and the most recent words THEY confirmed or reshaped for one marker
type, and say nothing else.

Governance dependency (OPEN): trajectory features are gated by `trajectoryEnabled()`
(`ELDER_TRAJECTORY_ENABLED`, `MARKER_CONFIRMATION_READY`, `CEILING_RATIFIED`). The ceiling is the
Appendix B synthetic-intimacy ceiling, ratified by Shalom Ormsby on 2026-06-30. R1 is designed to
sit inside it (no Elder-voice memory, no counts, no claimed change, no asserted connection), but
that reading is the designers' and must be confirmed by Shalom before the flags are flipped.
Until then the endpoint returns `{ reflection: null }` and the screen is unchanged.

## Decision

The return screen MAY show a seeker the first and most recent words they themselves confirmed or
reshaped for a single marker type, verbatim, dated and in static curated copy that claims nothing
about them; in every other case it shows nothing.

Enforced by `lib/returning/reflection.ts` (pure rules), `lib/returning/reflection.test.ts`, and
`scripts/check-reflection-register.mjs` (`npm run check:reflection`):

1. Source: `visit_record.markers_confirmed` only, scoped to the signed-in user. Model-proposed
   markers and declines are never read or shown.
2. Copy is static. No model writes or rephrases any of it; it is not spoken in the Elder's voice.
3. No counts, no claim that anything changed or progressed, no pairing of different marker types.
4. Silent unless two distinct confirmed values of the same type are at least 14 days apart and the
   latest is not simply the first again.
5. Gate: the route calls `trajectoryEnabled()` and queries the database only when enabled.

## Consequences

- No new table and no migration. One read-only SELECT (`confirmedMarkerHistory`).
- Dismissal ("Set this down") is per-session only; nothing is stored about it.
- Privacy: the seeker's own words appear on screen at arrival. On a shared screen a third party
  could read them. Mitigation is the dismiss control; a stronger option (tap-to-reveal) is a
  possible follow-up, not built.
- Copy is English-only, consistent with the existing myth-choice screen.
- Because the seeker chose the words, the reflection cannot be wrong about them; it can only be
  unwelcome. The dismiss control and the silence rules are the guardrails.
- Forecloses: Elder-voiced summaries, comparisons or trajectories built on this block. Those would
  need a new ADR.

## Provenance

Founder thread 2026-09-30 ("the user views their life differently after getting their reading";
"start with the return-visit reflection, because it needs the least new machinery"). Builds on
ADR-0009 (fail toward silence) and the Appendix B synthetic-intimacy ceiling.
