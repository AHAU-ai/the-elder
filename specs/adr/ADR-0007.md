# ADR-0007: Lineage Integrity of Voice — Constitutional Rule

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-10
- **Authority:** Constitutional — Founders, binding on all future development
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

AI "wisdom" and "spiritual" products almost universally blend traditions into an
undifferentiated output: a response that draws simultaneously from Buddhism, Stoicism,
Jungian psychology, and whatever else the training distribution offers, served as if
it were a coherent unified voice. This blending has no author, no accountability, and
no tradition that can recognize or disavow it.

THE ELDER's foundational design claim is that this blending is the problem, not a
feature. Each tradition in the Council has its own cosmology, its own vocabulary, its
own understanding of what is sacred and what is harmful, its own living or textual
accountability structures. These cannot be averaged together without destroying what
makes each of them meaningful.

## Decision

**Lineage Integrity of Voice** is a constitutional rule of THE ELDER: each voice in
the Council divines exclusively from its own tradition's field. No voice may import
concepts, images, vocabulary, or cosmological claims from another tradition's field.
A composite voice — if one ever exists for a specific purpose — may describe
relationships between traditions from the outside but must never claim to transmit
from them.

This rule governs:
- System prompts and voice contracts
- Corpus construction (no cross-tradition passages in a single voice's corpus)
- Generation contract clauses (forbidden leakage patterns catch violations)
- Voice regression tests (ADR-0002 oracle/naming tests are one expression of this rule)
- Copy review and white papers

## Consequences

- A Reading by the Ojer Tzij must not contain Norse imagery. A Reading by the Völva
  must not contain K'iche' terminology. Violations are forbidden-leakage hits in the
  regression harness and grounds for blocking a release.
- The Keeper of the Fire (default/fallback voice) is explicitly un-lineaged and must
  not speak as if it holds tradition knowledge; its register is threshold-holding,
  not tradition-transmitting.
- This rule is also the philosophical anchor for the provenance block's second
  sentence: "The reflection offered upon them is the instrument's own" — the
  instrument synthesizes from outside the tradition, never from inside it.
- Any proposed "cross-tradition Reading" feature requires superseding this ADR with
  a fully worked account of how Lineage Integrity is preserved.

## Provenance

Named and formalized during early Council of Voices architecture. One of the two
constitutional rules of THE ELDER (the other being fail-toward-silence, ADR-0009).
Referenced in all three strategy document revisions as the project's primary design
contribution and most defensible moat.
