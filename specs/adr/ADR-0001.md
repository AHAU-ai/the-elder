# ADR-0001: COSMO Permanently Retired — No Named Convergence Voice

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-10
- **Authority:** Founders (Yes-I Barber, Vincent James Stanzione)
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

An earlier version of THE ELDER architecture included a named convergence voice — COSMO
— whose role was to synthesize or summarize across the Council of Voices. As the
Lineage Integrity of Voice principle was formalized, the convergence voice became
architecturally incoherent: a synthesis voice either draws from all traditions
simultaneously (violating Lineage Integrity) or draws from none (making it an
ungrounded generative act with no legitimate field). Neither is acceptable for an
instrument whose central claim is that each voice divines exclusively from its own
tradition's field.

The name "COSMO" additionally carried cosmological connotations that implied an
authority the voice could not hold.

## Decision

COSMO is permanently retired. There is no named convergence voice in THE ELDER, now
or in future versions. A composite voice may describe relationships between traditions
but must never claim to transmit from them. The default fallback voice is the Keeper
of the Fire — unnamed, un-lineaged, holding space — not a synthesizer.

## Consequences

- No voice spec, prompt file, or UI element may introduce a convergence or synthesis
  voice under any name without a superseding ADR, which must address the Lineage
  Integrity problem explicitly.
- The Keeper of the Fire is the correct fallback for silence, threshold-holding, and
  any session moment that does not belong to a specific tradition voice.
- Any future AI-assisted session that proposes reintroducing a convergence voice must
  be shown this ADR before the idea proceeds.

## Provenance

Emerged across multiple architecture sessions; formalized as a standing ruling during
the Council of Voices build-out. Confirmed in the AHAU AI strategy documents (v1–v3).
