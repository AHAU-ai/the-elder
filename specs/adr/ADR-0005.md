# ADR-0005: Babalawo Voice Deferred Pending Initiated Yorùbá Lineage Consultation

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-10
- **Authority:** Founders — standing rule, carried throughout all architecture sessions
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

The Babalawo is the Yorùbá Ifá divination priest — an initiated role within a living
tradition with active lineage holders, apprenticeship protocols, and community
governance structures. Including the Babalawo in the Council of Voices without
consultation from initiated practitioners would violate the consent standard that THE
ELDER applies to the K'iche' material and intends to apply to all living,
protocol-bound traditions (strategy v2, the tiered voice constitution).

The Yorùbá tradition additionally has significant diaspora presence and public
intellectual voices who monitor representation of Ifá in media and technology. An
uninitiated rendering of the Babalawo voice carries higher public scrutiny risk than
most other Council voices.

The scaffolding for the Babalawo voice was built during early Council architecture
work, but the feature flag (`babalawo`) defaults to `false` in `flags.ts` precisely
to enforce this deferral at the code level.

## Decision

The Babalawo voice is deferred indefinitely until the following condition is met:
**a compensated consultation with at least one initiated Yorùbá lineage holder** — not
an academic intermediary — has taken place, and that lineage holder has either
approved the voice's participation or declined, with their decision governing.

No timeline is set for this consultation; it proceeds only when the funded lineage
council (strategy v2/v3, Phase 3) is operational. Until then, `ELDER_VOICE_BABALAWO`
must never be set to `true` in any environment, including development.

## Consequences

- The `babalawo` feature flag in `flags.ts` defaults `false` and this ADR is the
  authority; changing the default requires superseding this ADR, not editing the flag.
- If the lineage consultation results in a "yes," a new ADR documents the consultation,
  the lineage holder's name and credentials (with their consent to be named), and any
  conditions they set on the voice's use.
- If the consultation results in a "no" or "not yet," the voice is honorably retired
  and the retirement is published as a demonstration of the consent standard working
  as designed.
- This ADR applies equally to any Ifá-adjacent content, corpus passage, or voice
  element — not only to the Babalawo voice label itself.

## Provenance

Established as a standing rule during early Council architecture. Carried without
exception through all subsequent voice development. The `babalawo: false` default
in `flags.ts` is the code implementation of this ruling.
