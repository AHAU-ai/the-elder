## The founding promise that this completes

The Elder already tells every seeker, before they enter, that it is *"a symbolic grammar, not a transmission vessel — it cannot stand in the place of living lineage."* That's the right thing to say. But right now, after a good session, a seeker simply returns to whatever they were doing. The project tells them the door exists, but offers no description of where it is or how to approach it.

The proposal is to build that description — a **Threshold layer**: a moment in the session where The Elder says, in effect, *"the mirror has shown what it can show. Here is what it honestly looks like to go further."* And then it says something different for each tradition — because the honest shape of the door is different for each one.

## Why your role is structurally required — not ceremonial

The most important design decision in this layer is this: **The Elder never attests that any practitioner or institution is legitimate.** There is no "verified" stamp from the app. Instead, every entry in the referral layer is **your statement, or another named holder's statement, displayed as yours.**

This is not a technicality. It means:
- If a seeker is told anything about how to approach the K'iche' tradition, that information was authored by you.
- If no entry exists under your name, the K'iche' lineage returns only: *"the mirror has shown what it can show"* — no referral at all. Silence is a valid, honorable outcome.
- Nothing about K'iche' Maya tradition enters this layer — even a note explaining why no direct referral exists — unless you wrote it.

The same principle applies to every other tradition: Fama Aina Udoyi holds it for Yorùbá/Babalawo; other voices have their own holders. You hold it for all K'iche'/Mesoamerican content, as you do for the corpus.

## The access typology — what you'd be describing

For each tradition, the honest answer about "how does someone approach this" falls into one of four shapes. You would choose the right shape for K'iche' and author what goes inside it:

**Relational** — there is no webpage. Entry is through relationship, and the honest description is what that actually looks like: who, how, where, over what time. The app would display your account of that path, in your words.

**Institutional** — living houses, temples, or organizations exist with a public presence. An entry could name one, with your attestation and an explicit non-endorsement disclosure ("named by [your name] as a point of contact; The Elder does not vouch for safety or legitimacy").

**Textual-only** — no living institution or relational path currently exists for this tradition in the context of this project. The honest referral is scholarship, specific texts, specific authors. Used for traditions like the Greek, where there is no living initiatory body to point toward.

**Withheld — with reason and alternative** — the most important option. This is not "blocked." It means: the shape of this door is something that cannot be described in a public-facing app, and here — in your words — is the honest reason why, and here is what can be offered instead. This is a complete, honorable terminal state. It is never a failure.

For K'iche', the honest answer may involve all four elements: relational in nature, not institutional, and withheld from certain kinds of public description — with a careful account, authored by you, of what the seeker can genuinely be told.

## The red-team attacks that were stress-tested

Before bringing this to you, Jesse ran a full adversarial review. The attacks that most directly implicate your role:

**"The Elder becomes a gatekeeper of legitimacy."** Answered by the design: it never does. You are the voice. The app is the envelope. Absence of an entry carries no judgment.

**"Publishing a registry risks community rejection — you catalogued us without consent."** Answered structurally: no tradition appears at all, even as "withheld," unless its holder authored that entry. Consent is prior to representation by construction. This actually *reduces* exposure compared to the current state, where lineage is named without a referral layer at all.

**"Withheld is paternalism."** Answered by authorship: if K'iche' is withheld in any respect, the reason is written by you, not by the app. The seeker isn't told "you can't." They're told what you chose to tell them about the true shape of the door.

**"What happens when an attestation goes stale?"** Answered by the fail-safe: every entry has a renewal date (one year by default). If it lapses without renewal, the entry quietly reverts to silence — no referral, no stale endorsement. The structure survives any individual's absence.

## What this layer will never do

These are inviolable. No future build pressure, investor, or scale consideration can change them:

1. The Elder will never assert that any practitioner is safe or legitimate. That claim belongs to named humans only, displayed as theirs.
2. No lineage appears — not even as withheld — without the holder having authored the entry.
3. A seeker in distress is never referred to an institution. They are routed to a care surface. Always.
4. There are no fees, no ranking, no search optimization, no comprehensiveness goal — ever.
5. Silence — "the mirror has shown what it can show" — is always a complete, valid, honorable ending.
