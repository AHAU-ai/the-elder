## 4. Points worth your judgment

1. **"Release every pairing the fire holds for you? This cannot be undone."** It is accurate (deleting is permanent) and it is blunt. Keep, or soften?
2. **The recollection disclosure** is the honesty line: most counterparts come from the model's own recollection, not an approved corpus (D7 caveat). It is shown only on those pairings. Confirm that this is the wording you want a seeker to read.
3. **"Kept until you release it."** There is no expiry. Confirm that "until you release it" is the promise (it is: D9 also deletes a chain's pairings when its last reading is released).
4. **Not shown anywhere:** the words "myth entry", "ledger", "signal", "clause", or any storage language. The model is also told to say nothing about those.
5. **The reading prose itself** (what the Elder says when it offers) is generated by the model under the clause Vincent approved; it is not in this table.
