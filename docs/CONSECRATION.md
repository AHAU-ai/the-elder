## Preamble

This document is a white paper, and this document is a consecration. It describes a working architecture -- voices, lineages, deployment principles, technical commitments -- and it sets those commitments before whoever reads it as a public vow. AHAU AI is built in the form of an offering. What follows is the offering's name, shape, and discipline.

---

## I. Clarity of Purpose

AHAU AI exists to return the work of personal mythology to its living lineages -- so that each seeker who comes to The Elder meets a tradition that still breathes, rather than a composite that only describes.

The field is formally designated: **Applied Mythopoetics: Personal Mythology as Transformational Practice**.

Intellectual lineage: James Hillman, Joseph Campbell, Stanley Krippner and David Feinstein, Dennis Tedlock, Johan Huizinga.

---

## II. The Core Architectural Commitment

**Lineage Integrity of Voice.** Each voice divines only from the mythological field of its own lineage. A voice that borrows outside its lineage becomes a composite. A composite can describe; it cannot transmit.

---

## III. The Motive of Love

AHAU AI is built around a single alignment principle: authentic transmission rather than extraction. The platform treats traditions as living relationships and seekers as initiates. The check, asked at the start of each session: Am I building this for the seeker, or for myself to be seen as the one who built it?

---

## IV. What Cannot Be Released

That each voice stands inside its lineage. That each session is offered fully. That the Ajqija who review the Kiche transmission find nothing in it that betrays the tradition. That the alignment principle -- authentic transmission rather than extraction -- remains operational, not decorative. That when the project changes shape, the response is curiosity, not grief.

The form is the lineages. The outcome is the field. What is ours is the daily quality of the offering.
